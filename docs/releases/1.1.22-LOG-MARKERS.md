## Evidence and root cause

The sanitized diagnostic identifies installed **1.1.21.0**, one VRChat process, log creation four seconds after process start, modification two seconds before observation and **1,930,060 bytes**. It reports six each of `Entering Room:`, `Joining`, `Joining or Creating Room:` and `Successfully joined room`, and **zero `OnJoinedRoom`**. The latest completed sequence ends with three `OnPlayerJoined` markers. Earlier leave/disconnect transitions are present. No private usernames, local report paths, account IDs or real world/instance addresses are retained here.

The shipped reducer required `OnJoinedRoom` and cleared the pending full location at the name-only `Joining or Creating Room:` second stage. Either incompatibility prevents detection. This report is neither evidence of a quiet client nor a >5 MB tail failure. The separately reviewed tail-baseline repair remains included for its independently reproduced case.

## Minimal repair and trust boundary

Both snapshot and incremental readers now share the same forward transition reducer. A valid pending full `Joining` address survives the name-only second stage; `Successfully joined room` or legacy `OnJoinedRoom` completes only that current pending transition. Generic/orphan completions cannot provide a location. New `Entering Room:`, new joins (including malformed ones), leave/disconnect, process/log replacement and truncation invalidate the earlier state. The latest incomplete transition cannot fall back to a previous successful room. Consent/session identity changes remain enforced.

Event recognition is anchored to the Behaviour payload start using the existing bare or timestamped envelope syntax. Marker substrings inside names do not act as transitions. This is **syntax filtering, not authentication**: a world can forge whole multiline records. All existing automatic API presence corroboration, expiry, pacing, consent, ledger and final-HTTP guards remain unchanged. Logs alone never authorize automatic invitations.

`tests/InstanceInviteTests/LiveMarkerTests.cs` is **report-derived**, not a claimed exact captured log. Only marker order/metadata is report evidence. The full world/instance address, names and player payloads are synthetic. The timestamp envelope follows the repository's established Behaviour fixture format; no guessed unknown payload fields are presented as captured evidence.

## Scan budget and measured costs

The separately reviewed bounded scan remains **64,000,000 bytes**, with incremental append-only polls after baseline. Exceeding the initial or per-read budget returns unknown `LogScanLimit`, not a historical-tail fallback. Other privacy-safe detection codes distinguish process/session, freshness and transition failures. `LogNotFresh` describes only that specific condition, not this user's confirmed failure.

A local isolated production-reader probe on a **63,998,976-byte synthetic log** measured **643.9516 ms** initial scan, **383,292,360 allocated bytes**, and **384,360,448-byte process peak working set**. The unchanged poll took **1.246 ms** / **3,280 allocated bytes**; appended leave processing took **11.0979 ms** / **7,952 bytes**. A fresh reader at **64,000,001 bytes** rejected with `LogScanLimit` in **9.7189 ms**. These are one-machine observations, not limits or user-hardware guarantees; allocation is not retained-memory usage. Initial scanning is synchronous and may pause the UI. No freeze-free claim is made. See `performance.log` and the isolated probe sources for reproduction.
